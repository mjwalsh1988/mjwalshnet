On July 29 I did a round of search cleanup on FF Beacon. The big change was that each rankings format now has its own page, where before the same content sat behind 48 duplicate URLs.

I also added self-referencing canonicals to the plain pages, cut the crawl depth, and stopped passing internal links into a page marked noindex. Missing pages now return a real 404, after I scoped the loading boundary to the leagues section. The Beacon Brief got an RSS feed the same day.

Search impressions began climbing around August 20. I can't say how much of that came from this work.
