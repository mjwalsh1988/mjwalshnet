On September 24 the IDP work landed in four phases. Phase one built the defender data foundation and fixed the problems IDP leagues had. Phase two put defenders across the site and added an IDP guide. Phase three added them to League Pulse behind a switch. Phase four added an ESLint gate and an accuracy backtest with calibration, and then I turned the switch on.

The next day I closed out the build, fixed the KeepTradeCut sync and added alerts for failed cron jobs. The dedicated IDP rankings page for defensive linemen, linebackers and defensive backs followed on October 15.
