On April 20 the Price Exchange went into Dynasty Price Check as eight phases. Phase one added the Bollinger band columns and a signal history table, and phase two computed the bands and logged every signal change.

Phases three and four built the Exchange dashboard, with movers and buy and sell windows, and the player profile view with its chart, signal card and trades. Phase five rebuilt search and tore out the legacy code. Phase six redesigned the hero as a stock ticker, and phases seven and eight were a mobile and drawer pass and a visual polish sweep. An admin button to recompute the bands from KeepTradeCut data went in the same day.
